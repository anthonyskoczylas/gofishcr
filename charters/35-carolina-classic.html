Rates are per boat, priced for up to 6 guests, maximum 6 on board. Prices subject to change. To target billfish, book 3/4 or full day.

Included

  • Fishing gear, bait and tackle
  • Fruit, soda, juice, water and beer
  • Light lunch on 3/4 and full day charters
  • Captain and crew who speak English
  • Full insurance and Costa Rican permits

Not included

  • Fishing licenses
  • Crew tips (15–20% is customary)

Charter lengths

  • Half day · about 5 hours. Boats stay inshore: roosterfish, snapper, jacks.
  • 3/4 day · 6+ hours. Enough time to run offshore for sailfish, marlin, tuna and mahi.
  • Full day · 8+ hours. The serious billfish day.

Tips are not expected but very much appreciated. If the crew works hard for you, 15 to 20% is customary.
