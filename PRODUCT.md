# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Families and small groups from the US and Canada planning a Guanacaste week, usually 2 to 6 months out, on a laptop at night or a phone on the couch. One person is the planner (often a dad or the organised friend), one or two are serious anglers, the rest want the beach, the sunset sail and the zipline. They have never been to Tamarindo, they are nervous about picking a bad boat off a flashy site, and they want a human to tell them which one.

## Product Purpose
Go Fish Costa Rica is Steve and Liisa Quinn's charter and adventure agency in Tamarindo and Flamingo. They do not own the boats. They know every captain personally and match a group to the right boat for its size, budget and the season, then handle the rest of the week (tours, dinner tables, transport). The site exists to make a stranger trust two people they have never met, understand what a day on the water here actually feels like, and send them a request. Success is a request with dates, group size and a boat or tour attached.

## Brand Personality
Sunlit, plainspoken, local. Three words: bleached, honest, hosted.
The voice is Steve on the phone: short sentences, real numbers, no hype, a joke every so often. It should feel like the Gold Coast at 6:40am, not a yacht brochure and not an adventure park.

## Anti-references
- Navy-and-gold "luxury charter" sites with dark heroes and gold serif italics (Great Marlin already owns that lane in this portfolio).
- FishingBooker and Viator listing grids: filters first, feeling never.
- The 2026 AI landing page: hero, four trust cards, three numbered steps, three review cards, CTA band.
- Cream-and-Fraunces editorial minimalism.
- Their old site: cookie banner, "welcome to our new website" popup, emoji, stock sloths.

## Design Principles
- One day, told in order. The home page is a single charter day from 05:30 to sunset. The order carries the information; a visitor learns how a charter works by scrolling through one.
- Their photos, their water, their people. Real Tamarindo and Flamingo footage and Steve and Liisa's own library. No stock people, no generated fish.
- Light is the brand. Dry-season Guanacaste is white sky, teal water, hot sand. The page lives in daylight; dark is rationed to dawn and dusk.
- The boat is a decision, not a catalogue. Show the fleet at the moment in the day when you would be standing on the sand choosing one.
- Every request ends with a person. Every form leads to Steve and Liisa's inbox with everything they need in the first line.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast on all text, including over photographs (use scrims, not thin text). Full keyboard path through the day and the planner. Every scroll-driven effect has a reduced-motion fallback that shows the same content without scrubbing. Video never autoplays with sound. Touch targets 44px on the mobile book bar.
