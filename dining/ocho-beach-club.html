Ocho Beach Club

Tamarindo, Guanacaste

+4 photos

Nestled in the vibrant coastal town of Tamarindo, Ocho invites diners on a journey of culinary exploration, celebrating the rich tapestry of flavors found in Costa Rican cuisine. Situated amidst the lively streets and golden beaches, Ocho serves as a culinary oasis, offering a refreshing blend of tradition and innovation in every dish.

The menu at Ocho is a love letter to the flavors of Costa Rica, showcasing the freshest ingredients sourced from local farmers and fishermen. From mouthwatering ceviche and grilled seafood to hearty casados and inventive vegetarian dishes, each plate tells a story of the region's bounty and diversity.

Guests can unwind in the restaurant's laid-back yet stylish ambiance, where rustic wooden furnishings and tropical accents create an inviting atmosphere. Whether seated indoors in the cozy dining area or outdoors on the bustling terrace, diners can soak in the vibrant energy of Tamarindo while savoring the culinary delights of Ocho.

To complement the exquisite cuisine, Ocho offers an impressive selection of cocktails crafted with premium spirits and fresh tropical ingredients. From classic margaritas to innovative craft concoctions, there's a libation to suit every palate and occasion.

With its commitment to sustainability, community, and culinary excellence, Ocho stands as a testament to the rich culinary heritage of Costa Rica. Whether you're a seasoned traveler seeking authentic flavors or a local looking for a taste of home, Ocho invites you to experience the essence of Costa Rican cuisine in the heart of Tamarindo.
