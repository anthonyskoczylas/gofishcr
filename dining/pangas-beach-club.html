Pangas Beach Club

Tamarindo, Guanacaste

+6 photos

Pangas Beach Club in Tamarindo, Costa Rica, is a renowned dining destination that offers a unique blend of gourmet cuisine and a relaxed beachside ambiance. Located just steps from the pristine sands of Tamarindo Beach, the restaurant provides stunning ocean views and a stylish, yet casual setting.

The menu at Pangas Beach Club is a delightful mix of international and Costa Rican flavors, with an emphasis on fresh, locally sourced ingredients. Diners can enjoy a variety of dishes, including seafood specialties, tropical salads, and creative cocktails. The restaurant is also known for its commitment to sustainability and eco-friendly practices.

In addition to its excellent food, Pangas Beach Club often features live music and special events, making it a vibrant spot for both day and evening dining. Its laid-back atmosphere and beautiful beachfront location make it a popular choice for visitors and locals looking to enjoy a memorable meal by the sea.
