Sunset Catamaran Cruise – Tamarindo & Flamingo, Costa Rica

Sail the Pacific on a luxury catamaran while enjoying snorkeling, stand-up paddleboarding, swimming, and a late lunch (served around 3pm). Unlimited drinks—both alcoholic and non-alcoholic—are included. Relax as you watch the stunning Costa Rica sunset over the ocean.

Rates (tax included):

  • Tamarindo: Adults $108 | Kids 5–11 $74 | Under 5 free (no transportation)
  • Flamingo: Adults $108 | Kids 5–12 $74 | Under 5 free (transportation included)
  • Add $10 pp for JW Marriott or Pinilla pickups.
  • Private charters available upon request.
DurationMeet at 1pm | Return around 5:45pm

Pickup from Tamarindo or Flamingo on most tours. Hotels in Pinilla, JW Marriott or Westin may carry a small transport supplement, noted above where it applies.
