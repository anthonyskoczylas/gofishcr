El Chiringuito

Tamarindo, Guanacaste

+5 photos

El Chiringuito is a popular restaurant located in the heart of Tamarindo, Costa Rica, known for its vibrant and laid-back atmosphere that captures the essence of beachside dining. This is Steve & Liisa's favorite spot for sunset and happy hour, offering a perfect view of the ocean as the sun goes down.

The restaurant offers a range of fresh seafood dishes, traditional Costa Rican fare, and a variety of international cuisine. The menu highlights local ingredients and specialties, providing a taste of the region's rich culinary heritage.

With its casual ambiance and outdoor seating options, El Chiringuito is a favorite spot for both tourists and locals looking to enjoy a relaxed meal with stunning ocean views. They have live music different nights of the week, adding to its lively and welcoming environment.
