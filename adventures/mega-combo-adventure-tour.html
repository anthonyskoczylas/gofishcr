All-Day Mega Combo Tour – Guanacaste

For thrill-seekers, this all-day tour includes ziplining, river tubing, horseback riding, rock climbing, hot springs, and volcanic mud baths—plus a traditional Costa Rican lunch.

Cost$195 per person (minimum 4 people) | Private $225 per person (minimum 2 people)
DurationPickup at 7:30am | Return 6:30–7pm (2 hrs from Tamarindo)
IncludedTransportation, guide, lunch, all activities
What to BringShoes/clothes that can get wet, bathing suit, towel, sunscreen, bug repellent, camera, change of clothes

Pickup from Tamarindo or Flamingo on most tours. Hotels in Pinilla, JW Marriott or Westin may carry a small transport supplement, noted above where it applies.
