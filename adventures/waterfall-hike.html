Waterfall Hike – La Leona Waterfalls, Guanacaste

Take a moderate 1–2 hour hike (each way) through the jungle along the Rio Blanco River, crossing streams, caverns, and lush rainforest before arriving at the stunning La Leona Waterfalls. Swim, relax, and take in the scenery before hiking back. End your day with a traditional Costa Rican lunch at a local restaurant.

Cost$170 per person (tax included, based on minimum 4 people – private tours available)
DurationPickup at 8am | Return approx. 3:30pm (1.5 hours from Tamarindo)
IncludedTransportation, guide, entrance fees, lunch
What to BringWalking shoes that can get wet, bathing suit, towel, sunscreen, bug repellent, camera, change of clothes

Pickup from Tamarindo or Flamingo on most tours. Hotels in Pinilla, JW Marriott or Westin may carry a small transport supplement, noted above where it applies.
