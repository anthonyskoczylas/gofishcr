Café Tico

Tamarindo, Guanacaste

+5 photos

Nestled in the heart of Tamarindo, Cafe Tico offers a delicious journey through the flavors of Costa Rica. With a menu inspired by local ingredients and traditional recipes, every dish is a celebration of the country's rich culinary heritage.

At Cafe Tico, guests can indulge in a variety of Costa Rican specialties, from hearty breakfasts featuring gallo pinto and fried plantains to flavorful casados and fresh ceviche for lunch. Each dish is crafted with care and attention to detail, ensuring an authentic and unforgettable dining experience.

The ambiance at Cafe Tico is warm and welcoming, with rustic decor and friendly service that make guests feel right at home. Whether you're dining indoors or al fresco on the charming patio, every moment at Cafe Tico is infused with the vibrant spirit of Costa Rica.

Complementing the delicious food is a selection of locally sourced coffees, tropical juices, and refreshing beverages, perfect for starting your day off right or cooling off after a day of exploring Tamarindo. The knowledgeable staff is always on hand to provide recommendations and ensure that your dining experience is nothing short of exceptional.

Whether you're a visitor seeking a taste of authentic Costa Rican cuisine or a local looking for your new favorite spot, Cafe Tico invites you to savor the flavors of Costa Rica in the heart of Tamarindo.
