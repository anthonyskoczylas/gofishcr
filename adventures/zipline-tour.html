Zipline Tour

10 cables · 3.5 hrs · from Tamarindo or Flamingo

Zipline Canopy Tour – Guanacaste, Costa Rica

This thrilling canopy tour features 10 cables suspended up to 120 meters high and 580 meters long, with short hikes of 10–30 meters between platforms.

  • Transportation included (add $10 per person from JW Marriott, Pinilla, or Westin – minimum 4 people).

Zipline Option #2 – Tamarindo, Costa Rica

Soar across 10 lines and 11 platforms, including a 1,100 ft. cable stretching from one mountain top to another.

  • Transportation included for 4+ people. For 3 or fewer, add $25.
  • Pickup available at 9am, 11am, or 3pm.
