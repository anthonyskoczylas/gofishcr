Charter lengths

  • Half day · 5 hours. Boats stay inshore: roosterfish, snapper, jacks.
  • 3/4 day · 7 hours. Enough time to run offshore for sailfish, marlin, tuna and mahi.
  • Full day · 9 hours. The serious billfish day.

Tips are not expected but very much appreciated. If the crew works hard for you, 15 to 20% is customary.
