42' Lagoon Private Catamaran

Private sailing catamaran out of Tamarindo. Morning or sunset departures, snorkeling and paddleboarding gear, open bar and lunch on the sunset sail. Priced by request depending on group size and season.

What this boat offers

Morning or Sunset Tours AvailableWashroom onboard

Included

  • Fishing gear, bait and tackle
  • Fruit, soda, juice, water and beer
  • Light lunch on 3/4 and full day charters
  • Captain and crew who speak English
  • Full insurance and Costa Rican permits

Not included

  • Fishing licenses
  • Crew tips (15–20% is customary)

Charter lengths

  • Half day · about 5 hours, 7am to 12pm. Boats stay inshore: roosterfish, snapper, jacks.
  • 3/4 day · 6+ hours, 7am to 2pm. Enough time to run offshore for sailfish, marlin, tuna and mahi.
  • Full day · 8+ hours, 7am to 4pm. The serious billfish day.

Tips are not expected but very much appreciated. If the crew works hard for you, 15 to 20% is customary.
