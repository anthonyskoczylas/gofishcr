ATV Tours – Tamarindo & Flamingo, Costa Rica

Our ATV tours are one of the most popular adventures in Tamarindo and Flamingo. The 4-hour private ATV tour is a favorite at $170 per person, though we also offer 2–4 hour options. On the 4-hour tour, you’ll ride through beaches, mountains, towns, and back roads—all at your own pace. Stop anytime to swim, eat, take photos, or relax. Because the tours are private, you won’t be stuck in a group going too slow or too fast—you choose the pace.

Age RequirementMust be at least 16 years old to drive.
What to BringComfortable shoes and clothes that can get dirty, bathing suit, bug repellent, sunscreen, camera.
