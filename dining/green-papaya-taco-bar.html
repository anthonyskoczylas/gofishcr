Green Papaya Taco Bar

Tamarindo, Guanacaste

+4 photos

Nestled in the heart of Tamarindo, Costa Rica, Green Papaya Taco Bar invites diners on a culinary journey to Mexico, where vibrant flavors and bold spices take center stage. From traditional tacos to inventive creations, each dish is a celebration of the rich culinary heritage of Mexico.

At Green Papaya, the menu is a tantalizing mix of classic Mexican street food and modern twists, featuring a variety of tacos, burritos, and quesadillas bursting with fresh ingredients and authentic flavors. Whether you're craving tender carne asada, succulent shrimp, or flavorful vegetarian options, there's something to satisfy every palate.

The ambiance at Green Papaya is laid-back and inviting, with colorful décor, friendly service, and a lively atmosphere that captures the spirit of Mexico. Whether you're dining indoors or soaking up the sunshine on the outdoor terrace, every moment at Green Papaya is a fiesta for the senses.

Complementing the delicious food is a selection of refreshing beverages, including margaritas, cervezas, and homemade agua frescas, perfect for washing down spicy tacos and cooling off on a hot day. The friendly staff is always on hand to provide recommendations and ensure that your dining experience is nothing short of memorable.

Whether you're a taco aficionado or simply looking to spice up your day, Green Papaya Taco Bar invites you to experience the flavors of Mexico right here in Tamarindo, Costa Rica.
